## Read after the single analysis (interpretation only; nothing was changed)

- State C, both models: every MS-suggestive report on an examination whose application DIS is met is unqualified at the sentence level because of the code-written DIS sentence ("… the brain criterion is met."); the application's qualifier (RC-134) is the next, code-written sentence, so the report-level reading finds a clinical caveat in all of them, as pre-specified.

Gemini left it unqualified; Claude's Impression comment qualified it.
- Model-written statements: Gemini added regional (rule c) statements in 4 WMH 2017 and 1 MS reports that were already unqualified through the code-written sentence; Claude added none. Comments withheld after three failed checks: Gemini 15 of 168 and 4 of 121 in state C, Claude 4 and 2.
